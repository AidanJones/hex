# Death

You have a limited number of lives; more can be bought, but they get expensive,
so avoid dying. Check your remaining lives with `score` (or `help lives`).
Your corpse rots fairly quickly: go back for your kit fast, or ask for help.
Priests of Pishe (and a player Revival group) will often come to resurrect you;
being raised returns some of the XP you lost. Ask on the newbie channel.

# Staying safe

- Watch your HP (shown via GMCP / `score`). Leave a fight early rather than late.
- Some cities have local laws. In some places attacking a sacred animal
  (for example cats in one city) gets the guards involved, painfully.
- Before attacking anything, `consider <npc>` to gauge it (see `help consider`).

# Not being deleted

Inactive characters are eventually removed, with a grace period that grows
with how long you've played. Log in now and then to reset the clock.
