# How you get better

Progress is your skills, not your level. You earn experience points (XP) and
spend them to raise skills. XP comes from quests, jobs/missions, killing,
exploring and even from being logged in idle.

Skill trees: adventuring (climbing, swimming, health points), fighting, magic,
faith, covert (thievery), crafts, people (appraising, trading and similar).
Skill names are dotted paths, e.g. `adventuring.points`.

# Learning skills

You can learn from other players, from guild trainers, or teach yourself
(more expensive in XP). Self-teaching looks like:
`teach 5 levels of adventuring.points to <your name>` then `learn`.
Check `help teach` and `help learn` for exact syntax before relying on this.
Joining a guild usually makes XP go further for that guild's primary skills.

# Guilds

Each guild plays very differently: wizards and witches (utility spells, fast
travel), priests (healing, resurrection), thieves (safes, stealth),
warriors, assassins. Wizards learn spells via the Unseen University library.
The community wiki has a newbie guide for every guild; read yours before joining.

# Ways to earn without fighting

Odd jobs in Ankh-Morpork (deliveries, photographing locations, street repairs),
crafting, languages, player councils, clubs and newspapers.
Discworld quests are often puzzles or stories rather than "kill ten rats".
